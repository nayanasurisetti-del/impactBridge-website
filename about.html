ABOUT

Here at Impact Bridge, we strive to connect students with meaningful volunteer opportunities while helping local nonprofits reach passionate student volunteers. By bringing both together on one platform, we make it easier to build connections, strengthen communities, and create lasting impact!

HOW IT WORKS:

FIND

Browse volunteer opportunities posted by local organizations and discover meaningful ways to get involved in your community

POST

Organizations can submit volunteer opportunities for review to connect with students looking to make a difference

MAKE AN IMPACT

Students volunteer, organizations receive support, and together we build stronger communities through service
